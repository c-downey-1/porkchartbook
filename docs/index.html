Monthly Insights

Monthly Key Insights

  • Hog prices have collapsed to multi-year lows.
    On our chart, net barrow and gilt prices have fallen to around $63/cwt, roughly a quarter below a year ago, and the WASDE line puts Q4 near $53/cwt with only a gradual recovery through 2027. Outside measures tell the same story (the CME lean hog index is at its lowest since 2020, and analysts estimate farrow-to-finish losses near $49/head). With producers losing money on nearly every hog, efficiency gains that lift per-head margin matter more than ever: immunocastration, for example, improves feed conversion and lean yield and removes the cost of surgical castration, widening margins without waiting for a price rally. Hog prices → Forecast →
  • The cutout has dropped to a multi-year low, led by bellies.
    Pork Cutout Value is down to about $68/cwt, and the Primal Values chart shows the driver: the belly primal has fallen to roughly $66/cwt, about half of where it sat a year ago, with hams and picnics also sharply lower as bacon and processing demand sag. Cutout → Primals →
  • The gap between the cutout and hog prices has widened.
    Our cutout-to-hog spread has pushed up to around $11/cwt, near the top of its recent range, because live hog prices are falling faster than wholesale pork. That points to processors capturing margin while producers absorb the losses (outside trackers put packer margins near $46/head against producer losses near $49/head). View chart →
  • Feed is the one cushion for producers.
    On the Cost of Soybean Meal and Corn chart, corn has eased to about $160/ton and soybean meal to the low $300s, both close to year-ago levels and well below their early-fall highs after the harvest. Cheaper feed is partly offsetting the revenue collapse. View chart →
  • Exports are holding, but the competitive map is shifting.
    On the US Top Export Destinations chart, Mexico remains the largest market and has stayed roughly flat this year. The pressure is external and forward-looking: USDA has trimmed its export outlook as Brazil and Canada gain share in Mexico and the EU displaces US pork in South Korea. The US vs. Brazil chart shows how fast Brazil has closed the gap over the past decade. Destinations → US vs. Brazil →
  • Deep losses put herd contraction back in play.
    The Breeding Stock Inventory chart holds at about 5.88M head as of September, but with losses expected to persist into 2027, the December Hogs & Pigs report is the one to watch for whether producers start cutting the sow herd. View chart →
